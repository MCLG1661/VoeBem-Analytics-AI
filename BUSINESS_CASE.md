# Business Case Study — Aviation Data Engineering

## Business challenge

Raw aviation records need transformation into accessible operational indicators.

## Solution

Databricks medallion pipeline with Spark, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog and Genie natural-language analytics.

## Competencies demonstrated

Data engineering, analytical modeling, business intelligence and generative AI.

## Documented evidence

Four documented analytical domains: airlines, routes, origin airports and monthly trends; example Genie responses are included in the README.

## Scope and limitations

Demonstration using ANAC open data; no measured operational improvement or production SLA.
